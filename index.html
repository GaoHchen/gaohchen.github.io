gaohuachen712@gmail.com

I am a PhD student at Inception3D Lab, Westlake University and Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Anpei Chen. Prior to this, I obtained my MSc degree at the National Engineering Research Center for Visual Technology, Peking University, advised by Prof. Ronggang Wang. I received my BEng from Shandong University.

My research interests include spatial intelligence, 3D content generation / reconstruction / representation, neural rendering, and multi-view stereo.
