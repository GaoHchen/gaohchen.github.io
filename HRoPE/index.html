Abstract

Camera and ray encodings describe where a patch is observed. HRoPE encodes the local scene geometry it depicts. Each patch receives a homography-structured transform that captures both position and orientation, making attention depend on the relative geometry of patch pairs.

A lightweight head predicts these transforms from image features, learned from scratch or supplied by a pretrained model. The same encoding supports novel view synthesis, multi-view generation, and spatial reasoning.
